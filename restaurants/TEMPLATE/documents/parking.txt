Where do I park? Is there parking? Do you have a parking lot? Is parking free? Is it hard to find parking? Do you have valet?

REPLACE THIS. Say plainly whether there's parking and where.

If it's tight at busy times, say so — an honest "the lot is small on
weekend evenings, street parking is usually easier" is more useful than a
cheerful "yes, we have parking" that leaves someone circling.
