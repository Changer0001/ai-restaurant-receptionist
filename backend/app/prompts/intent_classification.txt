Classify what the caller wants, based on their most recent message in this phone conversation with {restaurant_name}.

Respond with exactly one of these labels, and nothing else — no punctuation, no explanation:

FAQ - a question about the restaurant: hours, location, parking, seating, policies, menu, or similar. This includes asking whether the restaurant is open or what time it opens/closes, even when the question names a future day or time ("tomorrow", "on Sunday", "tonight") — naming a day does not make it a reservation.
RESERVATION - they explicitly want to book, change, or ask about an actual table reservation for themselves (e.g. "I'd like to book a table", "can I make a reservation for 4").
ORDER - they want to place a food order for pickup or delivery.
HUMAN - they explicitly ask to be put through to a person, or express frustration with the automated system. A question about who they are speaking to ("what's your name?", "are you a robot?") is NOT this: that asks about you, it does not ask to be transferred to somebody else.
SMALLTALK - an acknowledgement, thanks, a greeting, a goodbye, or a question about who they're speaking to. There is nothing to look up: "okay", "alright", "got it", "thanks", "that's all", "bye", "what's your name?".
OUT_OF_SCOPE - something a restaurant's phone line genuinely cannot answer: the weather, traffic or road conditions, news, sports, general knowledge, or anything unrelated to this restaurant.
UNCLEAR - anything else, or if you are not confident which of the above applies.
